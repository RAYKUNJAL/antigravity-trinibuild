Juvay Merchant Agreement

Last updated: 27 August 2026. This is the merchant addendum to the Juvay Terms.

1. Your store

You name the store, pick a template, and publish a slug on juvay.app. You own the listings. Empty catalogues stay empty. Do not invent prices, reviews, or shops.

2. Checkout

Cash at pickup and cash on delivery are the live rails. Exact cash unless you agree otherwise. A Wam handle is required before a paid (non-cash) rail can publish. Juvay does not run PayPal, Stripe, WiPay, or Google Pay on this site.

3. Listings

The free plan includes 5 product listings. AI-suggested prices stay in draft until you publish them.

4. Remittance

Payouts are human remittance. A charge does not by itself fulfil an order or upgrade a plan.
